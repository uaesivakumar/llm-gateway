# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people.

## What is expected

- Give and take feedback graciously. A review comment is about the change, not the author.
- Be welcoming to people whose experience, background, native language or toolchain
  differs from yours.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a contribution. A "no" to a feature is not a
  judgement of the person proposing it.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sustained disruption of discussion,
publishing others' private information, or any conduct that would reasonably make someone
unwelcome here.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, and to
public spaces when someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **siva@sivakumar.ai**. Reports will be handled discreetly. Maintainers
may edit or remove contributions, or block accounts, where conduct is inappropriate.

This document is adapted in spirit from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), condensed for a project of
this size.
